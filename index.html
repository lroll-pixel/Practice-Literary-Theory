Applying the 4 Critical Lenses to Shirley Jackson's Anchor Text

📋 Assignment Instructions

🏷️ Anchor Text Spotlight: Shirley Jackson's "The Lottery" (1948)

First, read Shirley Jackson’s "The Lottery."

Second, complete the 4-Quadrant Matrix below.

For each of the four critical lenses, write a 3–4 sentence response that applies the analytical engine: WHAT → HOW → WHY.

Your 3-Step Analytical Engine for Each Quadrant:
  1. WHAT: Identify the specific element or detail highlighted by the lens.
  2. HOW: Embed one direct quote showing where this detail appears in the text.
  3. WHY: Explain how viewing the text through this lens changes our understanding of the work's larger meaning.

📐 GOING BEYOND: The Analytical Engine Using WHAT → HOW → WHY

To construct college-level literary analysis, move past simply labeling literary devices. Every strong analytical claim follows a 3-step sequence:

Step Diagnostic Question Example (Level Progression)
1. WHAT What specific element or detail does your lens highlight? Basic: "Jackson uses the black box as a symbol."
2. HOW How does the author construct this through specific evidence/devices? Stronger: "Jackson highlights the chipped paint and faded wood of the black box to show how ancient the ritual is."
3. WHY Why does this choice matter, and what does it reveal about the theme? College-Level: "By emphasizing the physical decay of the box, Jackson demonstrates that society blindly adheres to oppressive traditions long after their original purpose has been forgotten."

📝 Student Response Template

Fill out each quadrant completely:

🔬 Quadrant 1: Formalist Lens (The Microscope)

Prompt: Focus on one specific formal element (e.g., the symbolism of the black box/slips of paper, the peaceful rural setting description, or ironic diction).

🕰️ Quadrant 2: Historical / Biographical Lens (The Time Machine)

Prompt: How do post-WWII anxieties about social conformity, mob mentality, or Jackson's personal experience with small-town judgment inform the village's ritual?

🧠 Quadrant 3: Psychoanalytic Lens (The X-Ray)

Prompt: Analyze the villagers' subconscious defense mechanisms (denial, laughing off tension, scapegoating Tessie Hutchinson) to cope with collective violence.

💰 Quadrant 4: Marxist / Socio-Economic Lens (The Money & Power Lens)

Prompt: Examine how civic/business leaders (Mr. Summers and Mr. Graves) control the ritual mechanics to preserve social order and authority.
